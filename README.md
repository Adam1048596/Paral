 Users begin by completing a comprehensive onboarding questionnaire that captures skin type, concerns, lifestyle, and goals—this data feeds a recommendation engine that generates a fully personalized AM/PM routine using products from PARAL’s catalog. Every recommended product links to a deep educational product page with clear explanations, ingredient insights, usage guides, and visual aids, empowering users to make informed choices. From there, users can purchase products directly through the integrated shop, which supports local payment methods (Cash on Delivery, bank transfer, and eventually CMI) and fast shipping across Morocco via partners chronodiali.ma. and you can get your Paral routine shipping to your local Parapharmacy, Every purchase and completed routine action contributes to a built‑in loyalty system, where users earn points, unlock badges, and maintain streaks—encouraging long‑term engagement and repeat purchases. This tightly integrated flywheel (education → personalization → purchase → loyalty) not only builds trust and reduces decision fatigue for customers, but also increases lifetime value and creates a scalable, defensible business model tailored specifically to the Moroccan market.
